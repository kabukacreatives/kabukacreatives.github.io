How to film student stories

01
Ask after class, not before. When someone's glowing post-session, that's when the "can I film you real quick?" hits differently.
02
Use this prompt: "What were you dealing with before you started, and what's different now?" — that's the whole script.
03
Uma narrates the intro. "She walked in with ___." then hand it to the student. Keeps it consistent and builds Uma's voice.
04
30–55 seconds is the sweet spot. Data shows this is where your highest-follow reels live. Don't cut to 15 sec trying to be snappy.
05
Film vertically, good light, no ring light needed. Natural window light + lavalier mic = better than studio-produced content for this format.

Rules for this calendar

01
Promos live in Stories only, except the DM keyword CTA at the bottom of every reel caption. Never open a reel with the offer.
02

05
